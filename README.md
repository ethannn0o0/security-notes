## 📖 About | 关于

A growing, bilingual collection of my cybersecurity study notes: concepts, lab write-ups and practical checklists. I study security to **understand and defend** systems, and everything here is written for **legal, authorized learning**.

这是我持续更新的网络安全双语学习笔记，包括概念梳理、靶场 Write-up 和实用清单。我学习安全的目的是**理解并保护**系统，所有内容仅用于**合法授权的学习**。

## 🧭 Note Format | 笔记格式

Every note follows the same skeleton so it stays easy to review:

每篇笔记使用统一结构，方便日后复习：

```text
# Title | 标题
## 1. Concept | 概念          what it is, why it matters
## 2. How it works | 原理      mechanism, diagrams
## 3. Attack view | 攻击视角   how it is abused (lab only)
## 4. Defense view | 防御视角  detection and mitigation
## 5. Lab / Practice | 实操    legal lab steps and results
## 6. References | 参考        sources and further reading
```
